At the Water Isotopes, Climate and Karst (WICK) Lab in the Department of Geology at Occidental College we investigate hydroclimate variability from the surface to the subsurface in the present and past. 

Since joining the Geology department in January 2024, Prof. Sekhon and students have generated lots of new data at partner institutes, shared results at numerous conferences, conducted multiple field expeditions and created fun memories!
